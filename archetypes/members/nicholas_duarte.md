# Nicholas Duarte

## My Work

For this project, I was responsible for researching and designing three
brand archetypes:

1. Explorer
2. Innocent
3. Hero

Each archetype includes an explanation of the archetype, audience
motivations, visual and verbal cues, a real-world brand example, and four
original hero treatments combining different design styles and Cialdini
persuasion principles.

---

## Explorer

**Archetype Themes:** Freedom, independence, adventure, and discovery.

For the Explorer archetype, I used the fictional brand **WAYFARER**.

### Design Styles
- Swiss / International Typographic Style
- Grunge

### Persuasion Principles
- Scarcity
- Social Proof

### Hero Treatments
- Swiss + Scarcity
- Grunge + Scarcity
- Swiss + Social Proof
- Grunge + Social Proof
